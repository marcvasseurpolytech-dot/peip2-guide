Module 01

Les deux présents

Present Simple vs Present Continuous : habitude, action en cours, verbes d'état.

Le cours

1. Present Simple

Habitudes, vérités générales, états permanents. Marqueurs fréquents : always, usually, often, every day, on Mondays, never.

Ex. : Our quality team checks samples every Friday.

2. Present Continuous

Action en cours au moment où l'on parle, situation temporaire. Marqueurs fréquents : now, right now, at the moment, currently, these days.

Ex. : The technician is repairing the printer right now.

3. Verbes d'état (stative verbs)

Certains verbes décrivent un état plutôt qu'une action, et restent normalement au Simple : believe, know, understand, prefer, like, love, hate, want, need, belong, own, seem, remember, agree, mean.

CasRègleExemple
think = opinion Present Simple He thinks the policy is unfair.
think = réfléchir activement Present Continuous She is thinking about changing her major.
be + attribut, trait permanent Present Simple He is a calm person.
be + attribut, comportement ponctuel Present Continuous He is being very calm about it today (inhabituel).

Le causatif (have something done) est traité séparément dans le Module 02.
